# ADR-005: CRediT auto-mapping only for structured statements with exact terms

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-05-27

## Context

The PRD assumed the CREDIT STATEMENT is a structured "author initials → role
list" mapped to CRediT URLs by an exact lookup table. Scanning the corpus
(`examples/phase-3/scielo_markup/*.docx`) shows **three distinct shapes**:

1. **Role-keyed, CRediT terms** (e.g. 5523, 5313):
   `Conceptualization: Lopes DAPS, Nascimento IRN; Methodology: …`
2. **Author-keyed, CRediT terms** (e.g. 5136):
   `ATAJ: Conceptualization, Methodology, Software, …`
3. **Free narrative prose** (e.g. 5449, 5293, 5570):
   `"TTN Le and HDK Do prepared the material, collected, and analyzed the data…"`
   / `"Both authors participated…"` — the verbs are **not** CRediT terms, and
   5570 names no initials at all.

Author-name/initials formats also vary: `Lopes DAPS`, `Viana, A. P.`, `ATAJ`,
`TTN Le`, `KB Viandro`. Exact term→URL lookup is reliable for shapes 1–2 but
fails on shape 3, where mapping is genuine natural-language judgment. ADR-001
mandates: never silently emit a wrong role; favor prompts.

## Decision

Auto-apply CRediT **only** when the statement is structured and unambiguous:

- Parse the two structured shapes (role-keyed and author-keyed).
- Auto-apply a contributor's `<role>` elements **only when every listed term
  exact-matches the CRediT table** (case-insensitive, dash/`&`-normalized, e.g.
  `Writing - Original Draft` → `Writing – original draft`) **and every
  initial/name resolves to exactly one `<contrib>`**.
- **Prompt** (propose best guess, human confirms/overrides) when the statement
  is free prose, when any term is unrecognized, or when an initial cannot be
  resolved to a single author. Honor SPS all-or-nothing CRediT per document:
  if any term in a document lacks `@content-type`, none get it.
- A document where CRediT cannot be applied is reported, never silently skipped.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Structured auto + synonym layer for prose

- **Description**: Add a curated synonym table mapping narrative phrases
  (`"conceived and designed"` → Conceptualization + Methodology) to attempt
  shape 3 automatically.
- **Pros**: Higher automation rate.
- **Cons**: Open-ended maintenance burden; high wrong-guess risk on the exact
  cases that most need judgment; prose hits would be low-confidence and still
  prompt anyway.
- **Why rejected**: Cost/risk outweighs benefit for the MVP; revisit with a
  larger corpus (PRD Phase 2 tuning).

### Alternative 2: Always propose + confirm

- **Description**: Require human confirmation of CRediT on every document.
- **Pros**: Simplest gate logic.
- **Cons**: Re-introduces a prompt on every file — the MathML friction this
  effort exists to remove — even for clean structured statements.
- **Why rejected**: Defeats the central goal for the (common) structured case.

### Alternative 3: Defer CRediT from the MVP

- **Description**: Ship the other three tags now.
- **Pros**: Lowest MVP risk.
- **Cons**: Drops a PRD-scoped tag; the structured cases are already tractable.
- **Why rejected**: Structured statements are common and high-precision; no need
  to drop the whole tag.

## Consequences

### Positive

- High precision: roles are auto-written only when terms and authors are
  unambiguous.
- Honest about the limit — prose cases surface for human judgment instead of
  being mis-mapped.

### Negative

- Prose-style statements (a meaningful fraction of the corpus) always prompt,
  lowering the no-interaction rate for CRediT specifically.

### Risks

- **Initials resolution is itself fuzzy** across naming conventions.
  Mitigation: resolve against `<name><surname>/<given-names>` by building
  candidate initials (given-initials + surname); require a unique match,
  otherwise prompt.

## Implementation Notes

CRediT term→URL table is in `docs/scielo_context/jats/credit_roles.md`
(`https://credit.niso.org/contributor-roles/<slug>/`). Inject each `<role>`
inside the matched `<contrib>` after its `<xref>` elements. Track the
interaction-driver metric per tag (PRD success metrics) to inform Phase 2
tuning.
